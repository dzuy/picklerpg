## Player controls

Profile groups Blocked Players, Sign out, Delete account, and Help & support inside Account Settings, collapsed by default. Blocked Players opens a dialog with named Unblock buttons and loads the list on demand. Persistent guest sessions also have Delete guest account. Deletion requires explicit acknowledgement and password verification for registered accounts, is scoped to the authenticated account, and queues a durable, idempotent request. It is a request, not immediate deletion: the displayed processing deadline is 30 days. Cancellation closes the dialog before submission; after submission the owner must contact support to discuss a request. No existing account was deleted during development.

Report or block is available on Community player details, friend profiles and the opponent player profile opened by clicking their name during online gameplay. Reports contain a bounded reason/details and server-selected evidence (public design or the opponent's last ten messages). The reporter cannot select a different sender for match evidence. The server checks match membership. Blocking is symmetric for new contact, filters Community discovery and the team directory, hides message feeds, and prevents new invitations, challenge acceptance, matches, messages and nudges. Existing games remain available for finishing/leaving; cancelling or declining an invitation remains allowed. Blocking can be reversed by its owner; it does not grant ownership or delete another account.

The existing message profanity filter now also applies to player names/catchphrases and directory display text. Database triggers cover direct player writes, and Community projections filter legacy names. This is a basic English filter; human moderation and reporting remain necessary, including for evasion and other languages. Existing Community moderation remains separate from the creator's sharing flag. Deleting a moderator preserves hidden-player decisions.

The migration does not delete existing users. Do not run the deletion processor against arbitrary accounts. Keep purchase enablement/enforcement unchanged.

Hosted player-card publishing now requires a persistent session (registered or guest) and records each card's owning account under an owner-scoped v2 path. The database has no public write/list access. Cleanup removes that owner's hosted images before deleting the account. Older v1 card links have no recorded owner; investigate any legacy hosted cards from pre-release testing individually before promising complete erasure. Copies downloaded/shared outside PickleBash cannot be recalled.

## Operator responsibilities

Automatica Labs must check pending reports and deletion requests daily. Target a first safety review within 24 hours and process deletion including external-provider cleanup within the displayed 30 days. These are manual operations, not an installed background worker or notification automation. Do not launch with an unattended queue. All commands use server credentials from the selected environment and produce private operator output; never paste report content or credentials into analytics/public logs.

- List requests: `node --env-file=.env.local --import tsx scripts/admin/list-account-deletions.ts`.
- Verify one request without changes: `node --env-file=.env.local --import tsx scripts/admin/process-account-deletion.ts --account=<UUID>`.
- Process that verified owner request: add `--execute`. The script refuses accounts without a saved request, removes owned hosted cards, then uses Supabase's account deletion API. Database cleanup removes the account's players, history, saved games, coaching records, progression, social links, ownership, push registrations and shared match snapshots. Other users' accounts, private rosters and pack ownership remain intact. Shared matches involving the deleted identity are removed. Provider/payment dispute records and limited safety/request audit records may remain for their legitimate purpose.
- Remove the exact account UUID/person from PostHog (including associated events/recordings as supported), and remove/minimize its RevenueCat and Stripe customer metadata according to provider deletion tools and any required payment retention. A deleted identity must not be silently recreated by a webhook. Verify backups/retention and legacy hosted cards. Do not refund transactions or revoke another account's ownership as part of deletion.
- After those steps, record completion with the same processor plus `--execute --external-cleanup-complete`. This flag records the operator's verification; it does not perform external cleanup.
- List reports: `node --env-file=.env.local --import tsx scripts/admin/review-player-reports.ts` (oldest 100 unresolved). Review evidence privately. Use the existing trusted Community-admin hide action, or `--review=<report-id> --hide-player --execute` to hide the reported public player and mark reviewed. Omit `--hide-player` to mark reviewed after handling or dismissing with an appropriate internal record. Account-level cases must be investigated separately; a report alone never automatically deletes or bans a player.
