This build fixes player edits reverting or disappearing from Your Team.

Please test:
• Edit a player's name, appearance, and skills, then save and reopen the roster.
• Close and reopen the app and confirm the saved player remains correct.
• Edit while offline, reconnect, and check the sync status.
• Open the same roster on two devices and verify an older edit cannot overwrite the newer saved player.
• Resume an existing game and confirm its original lineup is preserved.

If a conflict appears, reload to see the latest saved player. The rejected edits are kept on that device for recovery.
