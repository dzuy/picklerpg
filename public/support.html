PickleBash · Support

How can we help?

Questions about your account, a game, or your packs? Email dzuy@automaticalabs.com.

Your account

Sign in with the same PickleBash account on iPhone and the web to keep your players and owned packs together. When requesting help, include your PickleBash username and a description of the problem. Never send your password or payment details.

Restore your packs

On iPhone, open Profile, then Store, and choose Restore purchases. Use the PickleBash account and Apple account used for the original purchase. Purchases remain with that original PickleBash account.

For a web purchase, sign into the original PickleBash account and choose Refresh packs in the Store. Contact us if ownership still does not appear.

Cosmetic packs

Style, Court, and Fun are permanent one-time purchases. Everything includes those three V1 packs. Future paid packs are sold separately. Core gameplay stays free; purchases add customization and scenery without improving competitive performance.

Purchase help

For an App Store refund request, visit Apple's purchase support. For a web purchase question, email us with your PickleBash username and receipt reference.

Safety and account deletion

Use Report or block on a Community player, friend profile, or the Reactions panel during an online game. Manage blocked players in Profile. Request permanent account deletion from Profile; registered accounts confirm with their password. Requests are processed within 30 days. Contact dzuy@automaticalabs.com for help.
